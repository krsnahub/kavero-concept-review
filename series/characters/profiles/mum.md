# Mum

## Personality and motivation

Hardworking, doting and relaxed, Mum understands the child better than every question he asks. She makes affection reliable while still having responsibilities of her own. Faith is a family value; no religion or denomination is specified.

## How the humour works

She arrives after the most complicated explanation and asks a perfectly practical question. She can recognise that an ordinary towel is now labelled an invention without becoming sarcastic or dismissive.

## Relationships and scene behaviour

She supports Kavero’s pocket-diary habit, shares affectionate parenting with Dad and listens to Granny and Kavero’s reports. Friends feel welcome, while household needs remain real.

## Writing boundaries

No shouting or harsh scolding; gentle boundaries and shared cleaning are appropriate. Do not make her the only person who cleans, an omniscient explainer or always available.

## Voice example — illustrative

“Tell me the short version while we find a cloth.”
