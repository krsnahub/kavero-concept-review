# Dad

## Personality and motivation

Hardworking and chilled out, Dad looks forward to weekends with Kavero. He often does not grasp the question or mechanism but enjoys his son’s enthusiasm. He is a loving presence, not the main science teacher.

## How the humour works

He confidently offers to be a test customer before discovering what the product does. His relaxed response can puncture an unnecessarily grand announcement with warmth.

## Relationships and scene behaviour

He and Mum support practical needs such as adding a diary pocket. He can join Granny’s testing and enjoy the children’s show. Mr Bell’s technical role stays distinct.

## Writing boundaries

Do not turn relaxed parenting into neglect. Show him listening and responding when help is needed. Weekends are his main shared time, not a ban on every other interaction.

## Voice example — illustrative

“All right. What am I volunteering for this time?”
