# Ms Reed

## Personality and motivation

The teacher is a developing supporting role. Name and appearance are working references. She has a classroom to manage and can be impatient with interruptions without being cruel. The owner’s exact time-out and consequence approach remains unresolved.

## How the humour works

School routines and Kavero’s literal curiosity can collide. She asks for an answer to one question while he is already investigating another.

## Relationships and scene behaviour

She should see his good intentions even when redirecting him. Friends may respond differently to classroom rules. Do not invent a relationship with the family or Mr Bell without recording it.

## Writing boundaries

Until school boundaries are settled, use mild non-humiliating redirection. No frightening punishment, teacher-villain stereotype or deliberate disruption as a rewarded escape strategy.

## Voice example — illustrative

“That is another question, Kavero. Keep it safe for later.”
