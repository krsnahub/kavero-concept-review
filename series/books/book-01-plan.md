# Kavero’s Brilliant Inventions — current book plan

17 stories remain. Original stories 2 (The Biscuit Alarm), 14 (The Doorway Reminder), and 15 (The Grand Invention Show) were removed by the owner. Remaining stories are renumbered; original numbers and stable IDs are retained in JSON. Final outline lock is pending.

Fozzy is the owner-approved puppy name. Existing target: 12,000 story words (~706 per story). Proposed revised budget: 800–1,000 words per story, aiming at ~850 × 17 = 14,450; not yet locked. No cover art.

## 1. The Bed-Making Machine

Original story 1. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Mum, Fozzy.

Kavero builds a contraption to straighten his blanket, but Fozzy settles on it. Granny pulls, Poppy doubts, and the invention needs to accommodate a very comfortable puppy.

## 2. A Pocket Too Far

Original story 3. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Mum.

Kavero designs a pocket big enough for his diary and every useful object. As Granny adds compartments, the pocket becomes harder to wear than the things it carries.

## 3. The Slipper Shuttle

Original story 4. Characters: Kavero, Millie, Toby, Granny, Fozzy.

A cardboard trolley delivers Granny’s slippers—unless Fozzy retrieves them first. Kavero gives the eager puppy a delivery job of his own.

## 4. The Page-Turning Problem

Original story 5. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Miette.

A hands-free page turner turns three pages, then none, while Miette sits on the instructions. The friends forget to ask whether Granny actually enjoys using it.

## 5. The Rolling Pencil Rescue

Original story 6. Characters: Kavero, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mr Bell.

A pencil keeps rolling off Granny’s table, prompting an increasingly elaborate rescue system. Mr Bell helps the children notice the slope, and Kavero creates a pencil parking space.

## 6. Granny’s Third Hand

Original story 7. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Mum.

Granny is holding so many project parts that Kavero builds her an extra hand. When the holder grips the wrong things, Poppy notices Kavero’s own hands are available.

## 7. The Plant-Watering Parade

Original story 8. Characters: Kavero, Pip, Granny, Dad, Mr Bell.

Homemade channels should water several pots at once, but one pot gets nearly everything. Granny pours, Pip predicts waterfalls, and Mr Bell helps Kavero rethink the route.

## 8. The Quiet Snack Carrier

Original story 9. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Fozzy.

Kavero pads a snack box so Fozzy cannot hear it travelling to the garden. The puppy arrives anyway, revealing that sound is not his only clue.

## 9. The Wind-Proof Picnic

Original story 10. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mum, Dad, Fozzy, Miette.

Everyone helps secure a picnic against the wind, including things people still need to use. Granny’s request for lunch becomes the invention’s most important test.

## 10. The Question Catcher

Original story 11. Characters: Kavero, Toby, Granny, Mum.

Kavero builds a funnel-and-paper machine to catch questions before he forgets them. Granny listens into the wrong end, and a simpler way of saving questions emerges.

## 11. The Sock-Sorting Slide

Original story 12. Characters: Kavero, Millie, Poppy, Granny, Dad.

A slide should send matching socks into matching baskets, but socks do not travel according to colour. Millie enjoys the deliveries while Kavero rethinks what the machine can sort.

## 12. The Bubble Parking Bay

Original story 13. Characters: Kavero, Pip, Granny, Mum.

Kavero wants to park a bubble long enough to draw it. Granny’s spotted glasses and a wet-surface experiment reveal that keeping a bubble still is not the same as keeping it forever.

## 13. The Rain Orchestra

Original story 16. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Dad, Fozzy.

Kavero arranges containers for rain to play a tune, then waits with Granny for the weather. The eventual performance is less orderly—and more interesting—than expected.

## 14. The Cat Detour

Original story 17. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Miette, Fozzy.

Kavero creates an alternative perch to lure Miette off the work surface. The cat ignores every invitation, then claims the spot as soon as the children stop trying.

## 15. The Hug Substitute

Original story 18. Characters: Kavero, Pip, Granny, Mum, Fozzy.

Kavero invents a cushion for Granny to hug when he is out. Testing it gives her what she loves most: an afternoon with him, followed by a hug no invention can improve.

## 16. The Lost-Diary Detector

Original story 19. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Dad, Fozzy.

A worried Kavero tries to turn a cardboard tube into a diary detector. Retracing his steps finds the diary in yesterday’s clothes and inspires a useful place to keep it.

## 17. The What-If Machine

Original story 20. Characters: Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mum, Dad, Fozzy, Miette.

A machine combines question slips into gloriously impractical invention ideas. Granny draws an unexpected combination, and Kavero opens his diary to begin wondering again.
