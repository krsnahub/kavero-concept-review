## Shared-world approach

Warm family home, making corner, Grandma’s kitchen, nearby park and school. Each story begins with a concrete want/question, develops an imperfect plan, reaches a funny manageable snag, and ends with a small discovery or repair. Vary structures so the stories do not feel mechanically identical. Adults supervise tools, heat and electricity where relevant. Values emerge through action.
