BOOK ONE · CONCEPTS FOR REVIEW

Kavero’s Brilliant Inventions

A curious boy, a pocket diary full of “what ifs,” and twenty wonderfully imperfect ideas. With his loving Granny and friends, Kavero discovers that an invention’s surprises can be its best part.

Ages 8–1020 short stories12,000 words plannedSparse black-and-white illustrations
A little help choosing the adventures?
These are proposed story concepts, not the final manuscript. Please send back the story numbers you love, anything you would change, and any stories you would replace. Character names are working names.
STORY 01

The Bed-Making Machine

Kavero builds a contraption to straighten his blanket, but Biscuit settles on it. Granny pulls, Poppy doubts, and the invention needs to accommodate a very comfortable puppy.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Mum, Biscuit
STORY 02

The Biscuit Alarm

An alarm should announce when the biscuit tin opens, but it rattles whenever Granny nudges the table. Biscuit decides every alarm must mean a treat.

Characters Kavero, Pip, Granny, Dad, Biscuit
STORY 03

A Pocket Too Far

Kavero designs a pocket big enough for his diary and every useful object. As Granny adds compartments, the pocket becomes harder to wear than the things it carries.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Mum
STORY 04

The Slipper Shuttle

A cardboard trolley delivers Granny’s slippers—unless Biscuit retrieves them first. Kavero gives the eager puppy a delivery job of his own.

Characters Kavero, Millie, Toby, Granny, Biscuit
STORY 05

The Page-Turning Problem

A hands-free page turner turns three pages, then none, while Miette sits on the instructions. The friends forget to ask whether Granny actually enjoys using it.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Miette
STORY 06

The Rolling Pencil Rescue

A pencil keeps rolling off Granny’s table, prompting an increasingly elaborate rescue system. Mr Bell helps the children notice the slope, and Kavero creates a pencil parking space.

Characters Kavero, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mr Bell
STORY 07

Granny’s Third Hand

Granny is holding so many project parts that Kavero builds her an extra hand. When the holder grips the wrong things, Poppy notices Kavero’s own hands are available.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Mum
STORY 08

The Plant-Watering Parade

Homemade channels should water several pots at once, but one pot gets nearly everything. Granny pours, Pip predicts waterfalls, and Mr Bell helps Kavero rethink the route.

Characters Kavero, Pip, Granny, Dad, Mr Bell
STORY 09

The Quiet Snack Carrier

Kavero pads a snack box so Biscuit cannot hear it travelling to the garden. The puppy arrives anyway, revealing that sound is not his only clue.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Biscuit
STORY 10

The Wind-Proof Picnic

Everyone helps secure a picnic against the wind, including things people still need to use. Granny’s request for lunch becomes the invention’s most important test.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mum, Dad, Biscuit, Miette
STORY 11

The Question Catcher

Kavero builds a funnel-and-paper machine to catch questions before he forgets them. Granny listens into the wrong end, and a simpler way of saving questions emerges.

Characters Kavero, Toby, Granny, Mum
STORY 12

The Sock-Sorting Slide

A slide should send matching socks into matching baskets, but socks do not travel according to colour. Millie enjoys the deliveries while Kavero rethinks what the machine can sort.

Characters Kavero, Millie, Poppy, Granny, Dad
STORY 13

The Bubble Parking Bay

Kavero wants to park a bubble long enough to draw it. Granny’s spotted glasses and a wet-surface experiment reveal that keeping a bubble still is not the same as keeping it forever.

Characters Kavero, Pip, Granny, Mum
STORY 14

The Doorway Reminder

A flipping doorway sign reminds Kavero to check for his diary, but it also flips for everyone else. The mechanism works; choosing who it reminds is the puzzle.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Mr Bell
STORY 15

The Grand Invention Show

The friends exhibit their inventions on a stand that keeps showing the wrong side. Granny’s mistimed demonstration turns the event into a funny, honest celebration of unfinished ideas.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mum, Dad, Mr Bell
STORY 16

The Rain Orchestra

Kavero arranges containers for rain to play a tune, then waits with Granny for the weather. The eventual performance is less orderly—and more interesting—than expected.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Granny, Dad, Biscuit
STORY 17

The Cat Detour

Kavero creates an alternative perch to lure Miette off the work surface. The cat ignores every invitation, then claims the spot as soon as the children stop trying.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Miette, Biscuit
STORY 18

The Hug Substitute

Kavero invents a cushion for Granny to hug when he is out. Testing it gives her what she loves most: an afternoon with him, followed by a hug no invention can improve.

Characters Kavero, Pip, Granny, Mum, Biscuit
STORY 19

The Lost-Diary Detector

A worried Kavero tries to turn a cardboard tube into a diary detector. Retracing his steps finds the diary in yesterday’s clothes and inspires a useful place to keep it.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Toby, Granny, Dad, Biscuit
STORY 20

The What-If Machine

A machine combines question slips into gloriously impractical invention ideas. Granny draws an unexpected combination, and Kavero opens his diary to begin wondering again.

Characters Kavero, Poppy, Pip, Toby, Granny, Mum, Dad, Biscuit, Miette
Meet the regulars

Poppy is Kavero’s mellow, sceptical best friend. Pip eagerly adds even wilder questions. Toby is the cautious peacemaker. Millie is a younger cousin who visits occasionally. Granny is his affectionate, willing assistant; Mr Bell is the patient neighbour who repairs things. Biscuit is Kavero’s enthusiastic puppy, and Miette is Poppy’s wonderfully unimpressed cat.

Granny appears throughout. Only four stories bring Kavero and all three friends together. The diary holds questions and curiosities, rather than daily-event entries.
